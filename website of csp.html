Understanding Social Media

Every platform carries both opportunity and risk — knowing both is the first step.

  • Communication with others
  • Knowledge sharing
  • Career opportunities
  • Community awareness
  • Creativity & expression

❌ Risks

  • Fake news & misinformation
  • Cyberbullying
  • Privacy problems
  • Online scams
  • Excessive usage
  • Emotional impact

Protect Yourself Online

Practical habits that keep your accounts and identity safe.

🔑

Password Safety

Use strong, unique passwords. Never share them or reuse across sites.

👁

Privacy

Check privacy settings and be careful with location and personal info.

🚫

Scam Awareness

Avoid suspicious links, never share OTPs, verify unknown requests.

👤

Fake Accounts

Check profile details and be cautious with unknown or new accounts.

Before You Share, Ask Yourself…

✓ Is this information true?
✓ Who created it?
✓ Is the source reliable?
✓ Is it recent?
✓ Could this hurt someone?
✓ Am I protecting privacy?
✓ Is sharing necessary?
Pause. Verify. Think. Then Share.

Be a Responsible Digital Citizen

✅ Do

  • Verify information
  • Respect others
  • Protect privacy
  • Use strong passwords
  • Report harmful content
  • Maintain healthy screen time

❌ Don't

  • Spread rumours
  • Share private information
  • Trust every forwarded message
  • Click suspicious links
  • Cyberbully
  • Spend excessive time online
